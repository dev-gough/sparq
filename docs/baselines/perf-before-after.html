§ 08 ·

What moved the needle

Narrative

Phase 1 upgraded Next and stripped dead packages. First Load JS stayed flat — expected; those packages were already tree-shaken out of client bundles.

Phase 2 removed layout cookies() theme FOUC and flipped 22 of 22 marketing routes to static. Lab CLS spiked until search-param islands landed.

Phase 3 carried the bulk of the JS cut: Firebase idle-split, Motion entrance removal, server Footer, lucide-only icons, legal content split. Home First Load hit the ≤130 kB budget at 124 kB.

Final stretch removed remaining Motion sitewide, fixed oversized images (sizes + WebP heroes), closed /resources CLS, added experimental view transitions (shared JS rose to 115 kB — home now 137 kB), and completed the a11y pass.

Still open (ops-gated): public media cull, API/secrets review, standalone output. Optional polish: DropdownMenu keyboard, modal focus trap, live axe/LHCI a11y gates.

Method note: Desktop Chromium lab with fixed throttling, three runs per URL, medians reported. Production next start on localhost. Field mobile LCP will differ.
